# Chronology rules

- Every training year must be strictly less than target year T.
- Target FCOVER is label-only at evaluation and cannot enter fitting, feature construction, window definition, AOI selection, QA selection, or block assignment.
- Target NDVI is allowed only for model application.
- Block IDs depend only on geometry and the frozen block grid and are stable across years.
- 2024 is retrospective; 2025 is the independent future-year target.
- The optional 2021-2024→2025 window is disabled and excluded from primary contrasts.
