# Leakage rules

Automated gates reject a history year `>= target_year`, reserve blocks in GroupKFold/LOYO, target-label fields in the window contract, and AOI-selection paths containing model/result/metric directories. The final refit explicitly permits Development+reserve. 2025 FCOVER may be read only by a terminal evaluator after predictions are fixed.

AOI selection and baseline FCOVER preprocessing are frozen before scientific execution. No target result may change preprocessing, temporal support, observation-count threshold, blocks, or statistical family.
