# Reference gap plan

No bibliography expansion was performed in this design-only revision. Before post-results manuscript integration, add and verify primary literature for the following purposes:

| Planned section | Needed source type | Intended support |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial/environmental generalization | Remote-sensing validation studies that replicate methods across heterogeneous environmental domains | Why a single AOI cannot establish environmental transferability. |
| Structured spatial validation | Primary methodological sources on spatially dependent samples and blocked cross-validation | Why GroupKFold is a transfer diagnostic rather than random-split accuracy. |
| Rolling-origin / forward temporal validation | Primary forecasting or remote-sensing temporal-validation methods | Why training must precede target years and why rolling origins answer chronological transfer. |
| Temporal non-stationarity | Ecological/remote-sensing studies on interannual phenology, surface conditions, or calibration drift | Why more historical years need not monotonically improve future agreement. |
| High-altitude Qinghai Plateau vegetation | Regional, peer-reviewed studies on plateau vegetation, terrain, climate, and sparse-cover environments | Context for selected contrasting study areas without implying province-wide representation. |

Only verified references should be added. The existing Copernicus PUM, ATBD, and validation report remain the primary sources for FCOVER product semantics.
