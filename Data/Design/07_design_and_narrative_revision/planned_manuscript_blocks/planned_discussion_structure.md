# Planned Discussion structure (post-results only)

1. **Environmental generalization across AOIs.** Ask which agreement patterns replicate or diverge across the selected domains; do not infer exhaustive plateau coverage.
2. **Historical calibration length and temporal non-stationarity.** Interpret whether added history helped, harmed, or had conditional effects without assuming monotonicity.
3. **What spatial/interannual diagnostics indicate about forward transfer.** Contrast complementary validation roles rather than treating diagnostics as model-selection races.
4. **Common-support interpretation and sensor-specific differences.** Separate support control from residual sensor, sampling, spectral, QA, and environmental effects.
5. **Operational use of FCOVER as a product reference.** Reiterate that FCOVER is not field ground truth and distinguish QFLAG/NOBS from the derived validity domain.
6. **Limitations.** Retain field-reference, temporal-coverage, selected-domain, and source-processing limitations.

Each subsection must be written after results exist; this structure supplies questions and interpretation boundaries, not findings.
