# Planned Multi-AOI methods (post-results only)

The original AOI remains unchanged as AOI-00. Ten candidates were fixed before model evaluation; three additional AOIs were selected using frozen environmental descriptors, comparable physical area, geographic separation, eligibility criteria, and deterministic environmental contrast. Selection did not use RMSE, MAE, Bias, R², model rank, significance results, or any 2025 FCOVER performance.

All AOIs use the same sensor preprocessing, nominal dates, common native 300 m FCOVER support, temporal rules, FCOVER baseline screening, spatial blocks, validation hierarchy, OLS specification, clipping, and reporting metrics. The four AOIs are environmentally contrasting plateau study domains within Qinghai and do not constitute exhaustive or statistically representative Qinghai-wide coverage. Results are reported by AOI, sensor, and target year; blocks are nested within AOI and cross-AOI interpretation is replication/consistency rather than iid pooling.
