# Planned research questions (post-results only)

1. Under a common 300 m FCOVER support, how consistently do sensor-specific NDVI-based FVC retrievals reproduce the Copernicus FCOVER product reference across selected environmentally contrasting study areas in Qinghai?
2. Does increasing historical calibration length from one to two or three years consistently improve forward-year FCOVER-reference agreement, or are the effects sensor-, target-year-, and environment-dependent?
3. To what extent do development-stage spatial and interannual diagnostics agree with subsequent forward-year performance across sensors and study areas?

Endpoint scaling remains a secondary operational baseline under a distinct information condition, not the primary novelty.
