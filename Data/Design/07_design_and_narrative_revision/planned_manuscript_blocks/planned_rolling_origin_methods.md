# Planned rolling-origin methods (post-results only)

Rolling-origin evaluation adds a chronological validation axis; it does not replace GroupKFold, LOYO, or the historical spatial reserve. Training years always precede the target. Primary rows are 2023 -> 2024, 2022--2023 -> 2024, 2021--2023 -> 2024, 2024 -> 2025, 2023--2024 -> 2025, and 2022--2024 -> 2025. The optional 2021--2024 -> 2025 window is excluded from the primary analysis.

The 2024 target is retrospective forward-chaining evaluation because it now lies within the historical period used elsewhere. The 2025 target remains the primary independent future-year evaluation because 2025 FCOVER is not used for historical OLS calibration. One-year histories have LOYO marked NOT_APPLICABLE.
