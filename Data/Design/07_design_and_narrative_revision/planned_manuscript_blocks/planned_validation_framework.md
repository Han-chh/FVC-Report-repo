# Planned validation framework (post-results only)

Use the following hierarchy as complementary validation axes:

| Level | Purpose | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Control support mismatch | Common native 300 m FCOVER support |
| 2 | Within-AOI spatial transfer | Spatial GroupKFold |
| 3 | Historical interannual transfer | LOYO within eligible multi-year histories |
| 4 | Historical held-out spatial diagnostic | Pre-refit historical reserve |
| 5 | Environmental replication | Same frozen pipeline across selected AOIs |
| 6 | Chronological forward transfer | Rolling-origin 2024 and 2025 targets |

Do not present these as six competing model-selection methods. The historical reserve remains diagnostic and ceases to be independent after the full-history refit. Target FCOVER remains unavailable to fitting and design choices.
