# Statistical plan

Primary reporting is per AOI, per sensor, and per target year. Blocks are nested within AOI and are not treated as independent AOI-level replicates. The three within-cell contrasts are history 1 vs 2, 1 vs 3, and 2 vs 3. Each uses paired two-sided t tests of matched 5 km block RMSE. Holm adjustment is applied to the three contrasts separately within each sensor × AOI × target-year family.

Cross-AOI discussion will emphasize replication and consistency, not unconditional pooling into one large iid block sample. No significance test is executed in this phase.
