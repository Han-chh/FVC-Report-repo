# Final Scientific Design Lock

## Locked primary protocol

Nearest-nominal non-overlapping temporal composition within inclusive +/-15-day support; earlier-date ties; no source reuse; cellwise median with a minimum of two finite contributions.

## Locked models and lineage

Canonical lineage: `repo/Data/Additional Sensitivity Analysis/Canonical Primary Nonoverlap 20260830`. Formal analyses are 72 Multi-AOI OLS, 72 Rolling-Origin OLS, and 48 DPM endpoint configurations.

## Locked historical windows, spatial scale, and inference

Six 2025 Multi-AOI windows and code-defined H1/H2/H3 Rolling-Origin windows are locked. The sole inferential scale is prespecified 5-km blocks. Inference uses paired block RMSE, paired two-sided t-tests, and Holm correction within the predeclared three-contrast families.

## Locked sensitivities

Aggregation order: `repo/Data/Additional Sensitivity Analysis/New Nonoverlap Primary/Aggregation Order/summaries`. Landsat aerosol QA: `repo/Data/Additional Sensitivity Analysis/New Nonoverlap Primary/Landsat Aerosol QA/summaries`. Non-overlap is primary, not a sensitivity.

## Locked claim boundaries and deprecated analyses

All manuscript claims remain conditional on the operational FCOVER reference, selected AOIs, retained support, configuration, and the 5-km inferential scale. Overlapping primary and overlapping-baseline sensitivity outputs are deprecated and excluded from manuscript use.

## Out-of-scope future extensions

New block scales, spatial-autocorrelation diagnostics, resampling inference, new QA or FCOVER-quality sensitivities, sensors, AOIs, windows, endpoints, models, metrics, and validation strategies are out of scope.

Any subsequent change to the above requires reopening the scientific design lock and rerunning the affected provenance and manuscript-consistency audits.
